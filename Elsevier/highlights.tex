\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
    \item A cross-model intervention benchmark separates citation correctness from citation faithfulness in open-weight retrieval-augmented generation systems.
    \item Cited-other interventions expose post-rationalization rates of 21.6\% for Qwen, 17.8\% for Gemma, and 9.1\% for Llama.
    \item ConflictBank shows that supplied evidence shifts model answers even when it conflicts with knowledge stored in the model.
    \item Hidden-state probes outperform lexical, citation-logit, and semantic baselines, reaching test AUROC values between 0.831 and 0.969.
    \item Activation patching finds internal model components linked to citation scores, but scaling them does not fix citation errors during full generation.
\end{itemize}
