\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
    \item We stress-test citation assignment under answer-phrase insertion in three open-weight RAG models.
    \item Cited-other documents receive 9.1\%, 21.6\%, and 17.8\% of recovered-statement citations in Llama, Qwen, and Gemma.
    \item Document position and source retention differ across conditions, limiting causal comparisons.
    \item ConflictBank answer--citation co-occurrence does not identify the source that caused a claim.
    \item Corrected probes trail simple baselines; component scaling corrects no generation-level citation errors.
\end{itemize}
